Scanned documents in Bangla used to be a dead end, you couldn't copy a name,
search a phrase, or paste into Excel. PDF Studio's Bangla OCR tool fixes that
in seconds, for free.

## What it does

The tool runs each page of your PDF through **Tesseract OCR** with the Bengali
language pack. If the page already has a text layer (most modern PDFs do), we
use that directly. If not, we render the page to a high-DPI image and OCR it.

## How to use

1. Open [Bangla OCR](/tools/bangla-ocr).
2. Drop your PDF.
3. Pick a language:
   - **Bangla + English**, best for mixed documents (default)
   - **Bangla only**, for fully Bengali pages
   - **English only**, if it's all English (faster)
4. Click **Extract text**.
5. The text appears in a panel, copy, or click download.

## Tips for best results

- **Higher resolution = better OCR.** If you're scanning, do at least 300 dpi.
- **Plain backgrounds help.** Photos of NIDs on tabletops with shadows OCR
  worse than scanned copies on a white background.
- **For mixed PDFs**, leave "Force OCR" off, the tool will automatically use
  the text layer for digital pages and OCR only the scanned ones.

## What it's good for

- Copying names, phone numbers, IDs from scanned forms
- Searching old document archives
- Bulk-converting scanned receipts to text

## Privacy

The PDF is uploaded, processed, and deleted within an hour. Nothing is shared
