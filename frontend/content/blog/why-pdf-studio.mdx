You've been there. You upload a PDF, click "compress", wait thirty seconds, and then
a banner appears: _"Your free trial has expired. Upgrade to download."_ The file you
just processed is locked behind a paywall. You close the tab and search for something
else.

Every major "free" PDF tool has a catch. Here's what those catches look like in
practice - and what PDF Studio does instead.

## The Catch List

### Smallpdf

Two free tasks per day. That's it. Once you hit the limit, you get a countdown timer
until midnight. If you're processing payslips for a small team on a Monday morning,
two tasks won't get you far.

### iLovePDF

Merging is capped at 25 files per batch, with a 100 MB total upload limit on the
free tier. Exceed either threshold and you're prompted to subscribe. The limits reset
daily, but they're tight enough to catch you on real workloads.

### PDFescape

The first use feels genuinely free. The second time you return, it asks you to create
an account. The third time, features that worked before are now labelled "Premium."
The goalposts move quietly.

### Adobe Acrobat Online

Adobe's online tools exist to funnel you toward a subscription. The free tier lets
you convert one file at a time, with a strict monthly cap and prominent "Go Premium"
buttons on every result page.

## What PDF Studio Does Differently

**No watermark on output.** The PDF you download is clean - no "Processed by
PDF Studio" stamp, no blurred pages, no corner badge. What goes in comes out.

**No daily task limit.** Compress ten files. Merge twenty. Run OCR on a full archive.
There is no counter ticking down in the background.

**No sign-up, no email collection.** You don't have an account here. There's no
login screen, no verification email, no marketing list you're joining by clicking
"Get started."

**Files auto-delete in one hour.** Uploaded files are removed from the server within
sixty minutes of processing. We don't train on your documents, we don't index them,
and we don't retain them for analytics.

**100 MB per upload.** Most compressed PDFs and scanned documents fit comfortably
within this limit. For larger files, split the batch.

## The 9 Tools We Ship Today

These are the tools available right now - all free, all watermark-free:

1. **[Compress PDF](/tools/compress-pdf-without-losing-quality)** - Shrink file size
   while preserving readable quality. Useful before attaching to email or uploading
   to a portal with a size cap.

2. **[Merge PDF](/tools/merge-large-pdf-files-online)** - Combine multiple PDFs into
   one. Drag to reorder pages before merging.

3. **[PDF to JPG/PNG](/tools/pdf-to-jpg-high-quality)** - Export each page as a
   high-resolution image. Useful when a recipient's system can't open PDFs or when
   you need to embed a page in a presentation.

4. **[Password-Protect PDF](/tools/password-protect-pdf-online)** - Add a password
   to any PDF before sharing sensitive documents. Uses 256-bit AES encryption.

5. **[Unlock PDF](/tools/unlock-pdf-with-password-online)** - Remove password
   protection from PDFs you own, when you know the password.

6. **[PDF OCR](/tools/pdf-ocr-online-free)** - Extract selectable text from scanned
   PDFs. Supports 100+ languages including Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, and Bengali. See
   the [OCR guide](/blog/multi-language-pdf-ocr-guide) for language details.

7. **[ID Card to PDF](/tools/id-card-to-pdf)** - Place the front and back of a driver's
   license, national ID, or employee badge onto a single A4 page. Useful for KYC
   submissions, onboarding packets, and visa applications. The
   [ID card guide](/blog/id-card-to-pdf-guide) explains layout options.

8. **[Passport Photo to PDF](/tools/passport-photo-to-pdf)** - Format a passport-size
   photo to standard dimensions on an A4 sheet for printing.

9. **[PDF Table to Excel](/tools/pdf-to-excel-converter)** - Detect tables in a PDF
   and export them as an editable `.xlsx` file.

Vercel's free tier, the backend on Render, and file storage on Fly.io with short
TTLs. There are no ads on tool pages. No affiliate links disguised as recommendations.

If a tool saves you twenty minutes, consider hitting the donate button. If not,
use it anyway - that's the point.
