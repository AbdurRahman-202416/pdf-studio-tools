Need passport-size photos for a visa, school ID, BCS form, or bank application?
The old way is to walk to a photo studio, pay 100–300 BDT (or ₹100–₹300, or
$10–$15), and wait. The new way: take a phone selfie at home, generate a
print-ready A4 sheet, and print it at the corner shop for less than 50 BDT.

## TL;DR

1. Open **[Passport Photo PDF](/passport-photo-pdf)**
2. Drop one clear photo of yourself
3. Pick size (35×45mm for BD/India/EU, 51×51mm for US visa)
4. Pick layout (Grid of 8 is most common)
5. Download and print on A4 photo paper

## Photo guidelines

The same rules apply globally:

- **Plain light background** — white or off-white
- **Face front-on, neutral expression, mouth closed**
- **No sunglasses, no hat, no headphones**
- **Even lighting, no shadows on face**
- **Resolution at least 1000 × 1000 pixels** (most phones easily exceed this)

If your background isn't clean, you can use a free background-remover (search
"remove.bg") before uploading.

## Layout choices

- **Single** — one large photo. Use if you need a single high-quality print for a digital scan.
- **Grid of 4** — 2×2 layout. Spaced and easy to cut.
- **Grid of 8** — 2×4 layout. Most common — gives you 8 copies on one A4 sheet.

## Print at home or at a shop

The output PDF is **A4 portrait**. Two print options:

### At home

- Use **photo paper** (glossy or matte) — regular paper gives a dull, washed-out result
- Set printer to **Actual size** or **100%** — NOT "Fit to page"
- For best color, print at **High quality** / **Photo** mode

### At a print shop

- Take the PDF on a USB stick or email it to the shop
- Ask for **A4 photo print** at **300 DPI**
- Typical cost: 20–50 BDT in Bangladesh, ₹15–₹40 in India, $1–$3 in the US

## Why this works

The tool centers each photo on the page with the exact physical size, allowing for
3 mm of border between copies so you can cut them apart with scissors. It uses
PyMuPDF for placement (the same library Adobe uses internally) so the printed
dimensions are exactly to spec.
