If you've ever needed a single A4 page with both sides of your Bangladeshi National
ID, for school, hospital, bank, or government office, you know the pain. Print
shops want exact card dimensions, photocopy machines crop the edges, and most online
tools either watermark the result or want money.

This guide walks you through doing it free in under 60 seconds with PDF Studio.

## What you need

- The front of your NID (photo or scanned PDF is fine)
- The back of your NID
- A web browser (mobile or desktop)

## Step-by-step

1. Open the [NID Combiner tool](/tools/nid-combine).
2. Drop the front image/PDF into the "Front side" box.
3. Drop the back image/PDF into the "Back side" box.
4. Choose **A4 portrait** (most common, vertical sheet).
5. Optionally toggle the "Front / Back" label.
6. Click **Combine NID**.
7. Click **Download**, you'll get a print-ready A4 PDF with both sides at real
   ID-card dimensions.

## Why the dimensions matter

Government offices and banks expect the NID to print at the actual card size
(roughly 85.6mm × 54mm). PDF Studio scales the images precisely so the final
print looks like a real card on the page. No squished aspect ratios, no
cropping.

## Privacy

Your NID never leaves the server beyond the seconds it takes to process. Files
automatically delete after one hour. No accounts, no tracking.
