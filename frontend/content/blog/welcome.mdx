PDF Studio is a free toolkit for everyday PDF work, merge, compress, split, lock,
convert, and OCR. Built for Bangladesh, free for everyone.

## Why this exists

Existing PDF sites are slow, ad-heavy, or paywall the features you actually need.
PDF Studio is the opposite: a small set of focused tools, free forever, with
Bangla-language support baked in.

## What you'll find here

- Step-by-step guides for each tool
- Tutorials on Bangladesh-specific workflows (NID printing, bank statements, e-TIN, etc.)
- Behind-the-scenes posts on how the product evolves
